Chapter 1: Introduction to Investment-Linked Policies (ILP)

Investment-linked insurance was introduced in Malaysia in December 1999[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). It combines insurance protection with investment opportunities, where policyholders bear the investment risk[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

  • International Names: Known as Unit-Linked Life Insurance in some countries and Variable Life Insurance in the USA[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
  • Key BNM Guideline: Bank Negara Malaysia Reference BNM/RH/PD 029-36 issued on 13 February 2023[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
  • Individual Income Tax Relief Limits (YA 2023):
    • Life Insurance & EPF Total: Up to RM7,000[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)
    • Life Insurance (Standalone): Up to RM3,000[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)
    • Education & Medical Insurance: Up to RM3,000[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)
    • PRS & Deferred Annuity: Up to RM3,000[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)

Key Product Mechanics

  • Free-Look Period: 15 calendar days from policy delivery date[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span). Full refund minus medical examination expenses incurred[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
  • Pricing Methods: Dual Pricing (bid/offer 5% spread, used 1999–2005) vs. Single Pricing (NAV-based, mandatory since 2006 for transparency)[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).
  • Fund Management Charge (FMC) Caps: Maximum 1.0% p.a. of NAV for Money Market/Fixed Income Funds; Maximum 1.5% p.a. of NAV for other funds[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
  • Riders: Unit-Deducting Riders (UDR - funded by deducting units, no extra premium) vs. Premium Paying Riders (PPR - separate premiums required)[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span).

Chapter 3: Disclosure Requirements

  • Product Illustration Scenarios: Scenario X (2%) and Scenario Y (5% or historical KLCI) represent hypothetical rates of return[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span).
  • Fund Fact Sheet: Contains objectives, risk level, fees, and historical performance (capped at 10 years)[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).
  • Annual Statement: Sent to policy owners within 2 months of the end of the financial year, showing unit transactions and sustainability test outcomes[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span).
  • Unit Price Publication: Daily Net Asset Value (NAV) per unit must be published on the insurer's official website[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span).
